What we keep

  • The nickname you type and a random player ID. Both are stored in your browser (local storage) so you can reconnect to a match after a refresh.
  • Room and match state (players, tokens, dice results) is held in the server's memory only while a room exists. Idle rooms are deleted automatically. There are no accounts and no match history yet.
