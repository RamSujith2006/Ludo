        How to Play Ludo Party

        1

        Players & Colours

        2–10 players. Each player chooses a colour: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Purple, Orange, and more depending on the match size.

        2

        Starting the Game

        Each player has up to 4 tokens inside their coloured home area (yard). The number of tokens adjusts for larger games.

        3

        Getting a Token Out

        Roll a 6 to move a token from the yard onto the board. In Quick mode, any roll brings a token out. Rolling a 6 also grants an extra turn!

        4

        Moving Tokens

        Move your token clockwise around the board according to the number shown on the dice. Plan your moves wisely!

        5

        Rolling a 6

        Roll a 6 to get an extra turn. But beware: three 6s in a row forfeit your turn entirely!

        6

        Capturing Opponents

        If your token lands on an opponent's token on a non-safe square, the opponent's token is sent back to their yard. You also earn a bonus roll!

        7

        Safe Squares

        Cells marked with a ★ star and each player's start cell are safe zones — tokens on these squares cannot be captured.

        8

        Reaching Home

        After completing the outer path, move your tokens along your own coloured home lane toward the centre. Only your tokens can enter your home lane.

        9

        Exact Number Required

        You need the exact dice number to move a token into the final home position. If the roll overshoots, that token cannot move.

        10

        Winning

        The first player to get all tokens home wins! In Ranking mode, play continues until the full ranking is decided. Disconnected players are replaced by bots.
