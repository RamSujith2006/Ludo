How to play
  • Goal: bring all your tokens round the ring and up your coloured home lane into the centre.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of the yard (or any roll in Quick mode). A 6, a capture, or a token getting home gives you another roll. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
  • Capture by landing exactly on an opponent. Cells with a ★ and every start cell are safe — tokens can share them.
  • Exact roll needed to finish. Stuck? Nothing to move? The turn passes automatically.
  • Away? If you stop playing for a couple of turns, a bot covers for you until you come back.
